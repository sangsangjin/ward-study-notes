Surgical Ward Meds

BPH 3B / 4B · 24 DRUGS

How to answer — 약 물어보면 이 순서로
  1. What — drug name + class (“It’s a …”)
  2. Why — indication for this patient (“It’s given for …”)
  3. How — mechanism in one line (“It works by …”)
  4. Side effects → nursing checks (“So I check … before / after giving it.”)

“This is Clexane, a low molecular weight heparin. It’s given to prevent DVT after surgery because the patient is less mobile. It works by inhibiting factor Xa. The main risk is bleeding, so I check platelets and look for signs of bleeding.”

⚠️ 이름 패턴 ≠ 100% 확정

  • -pine은 헷갈림: amlodipine = 혈압약(CCB), quetiapine = 항정신병약. 끝부분만 믿으면 안 됨.
  • -mycin은 다 같은 계열이 아님: azithromycin (macrolide) ≠ gentamicin (aminoglycoside) ≠ vancomycin (glycopeptide).
  • 패턴은 힌트일 뿐 — 최종 확인은 medication chart · MIMS/AMH · RN/pharmacist.
